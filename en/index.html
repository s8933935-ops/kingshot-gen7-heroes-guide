Who should you build first?

Wee & Woo

The Hero Roulette pick for free-to-play and low spenders. Aim for four stars and level 5 on the first Expedition skill.

Ava

Prioritize her with general shards and exclusive gear materials if you regularly lead attack or Bear Hunt rallies.

Charles

Prioritize him if you lead castle or structure defense. Compare him with your built Triton before switching.

Bear rally lead: Amadeus + Ava + Yang. For joining, Wee & Woo are a good first-slot option if your alliance calls for them. See the damage comparisons

Where to stop

Recruiting costs 10 hero-specific shards. From recruitment to 4★ takes another 465 shards. From 4★ to 5★ costs 600 more, or 1,075 total from scratch. Four stars unlocks level 5 Expedition skills, so joiners can stop there. Five stars matter more for rally leads and Arena carries. Wiki shard table · Recruitment costs

The Expedition exclusive gear skill unlocks at +2. Material totals at +2/+4/+6/+8/+10 are 15/50/105/180/275. Ava boosts rally Lethality when initiating, Charles boosts defender HP, and Wee & Woo boost defender Attack. Altema recommends Charles but describes his HP skill as working on attack as well. The hero Wiki specifies defending squads. Gear material costs · Charles on the Wiki · Altema

Where player reports differ

Early tests summarized by Strat Game Sloth favored Charles with a 60/40 infantry-cavalry split, Thrud and Saul. Triton worked better in some setups with Wee & Woo. Raw logs and trial counts were not published. Tester’s summary

DC Inside players recommend Roulette for Wee & Woo and general shards for Ava, while keeping built Yang for Bear Hunt. Reddit players tend to reserve Charles for dedicated defenders. Titan’s public video description gives a similar role split. WOSKing’s description advises free players to concentrate resources across generations. Joining a rally

The leader brings all three Expedition skills of each hero. For each joiner, the first Expedition skill of the first hero is eligible, with up to four skills selected across the rally by skill level. Wee & Woo’s Artillerymen gives up to +15% total Attack and +10% total Lethality. Follow alliance assignments. Combat FAQ · Wiki Bear Hunt guide

One Hededal example estimates +25% for Ava or Vivian, +21% for Wee & Woo and +20% for Chenko or Amane. Kingshot Intel does not treat Ava’s Defense Down as established Bear damage evidence. Stacking between the leader’s Ava and joiner Ava, or across multiple joiners using Ava, remains unverified. Joiner estimates · Alternative model · Stacking notes

Compare with your own roster

Try 10/10/80 infantry/cavalry/archers as a baseline. Test a heavier archer mix with the same troop count. Hededal’s example uses 1/2/97. Change one hero or one of the four accepted joiner skills, keeping the leader, troops and buffs fixed. Compare the median and range over several rallies. The model allows roughly ±15% variation from chance-based skills. Troop mix · Damage variability

Attack, garrison and Arena lineups

Bear Hunt

Lead with Amadeus, Ava and Yang at high investment. Use Wee & Woo as a joiner option. Bear Hunt details

Attack rally

The castle guide lists Amadeus, Triton or Charles with Ava and Wee & Woo. Its sample splits are 50/20/30, or 50/0/50 against infantry-heavy opponents.

Garrison

Charles + Jaeger + Sophia is the Wiki’s example. Start from 60/40/0 infantry/cavalry/archers and adjust to the matchup. Wee & Woo are an option in archer-inclusive defense.

In Arena, try Charles if the frontline collapses early, Wee & Woo if you need backline damage, or Ava to amplify damage. Check the replay before replacing a built hero. Castle Battle guide
